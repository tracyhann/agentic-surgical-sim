## What the authors say the videos show
- Legends: video 1, the lobectomy through the single incision; video 2, mediastinal lymph node dissection after the
  lobectomy (lower mediastinal, subcarinal, hilar, upper mediastinal).
- Article: left lateral decubitus, one 4 cm incision (5th intercostal space, anterior axillary line, wound
  retractor), 30-degree 10 mm thoracoscope, Harmonic scalpel, endostaplers, right-angle clamp with silastic drain,
  endo-graspers; one-lung ventilation (the operated lung is deflated); fissure dissected and stapled, upper-lobe
  bronchus and superior pulmonary vein encircled and stapled, minor fissure completed with staplers; 38 nodes.
