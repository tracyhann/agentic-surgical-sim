## What the authors say the video shows (checked 2026-10-08)
- Commons description / article video legend: inspection and laparoscopic removal of a rotated, gangrenous
  gallbladder; phases "derotation (0-23 s), decompression (24-33 s), cholecystectomy (34-42 s)".
- Article (PMC3259112): 89-year-old woman, acute cholecystitis; strongly hydropic, fully necrotic gallbladder,
  two-fold torsion around the cystic duct; gangrene extends into the cystic duct and artery; the gallbladder hung
  free from the cystic duct and artery with no attachment to the liver (floating gallbladder); no stones; hilum
  clipped (two proximal, one distal clip). The decompression instrument is not named in the text.
- So shot A (23.5-33.5 s) = decompression of the distended gallbladder; shot B (33.5-41.4 s) = cholecystectomy.
