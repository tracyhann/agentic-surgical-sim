## What the authors say the video shows
- Legend: diagnostic laparoscopy and the bile leak detected at the triangular ligament of the liver.
- Article: 58-year-old man, emergency laparoscopy for bile peritonitis (about 1 litre of bile); gallbladder, pylorus,
  duodenum and common bile duct intact; bile kept collecting in the left subphrenic space, and the leak was an
  aberrant bile duct in the fibrous appendix of the liver at the left end of the triangular ligament; closed with a
  few intracorporeal sutures. (The pancreaticoduodenectomy for the ampullary tumour came later; videos 3-6, which
  carry burned-in anatomy labels, were not downloaded.)
- Seen in the video (2026-10-08): liver lobes and bile-stained fat (0-11 s), gauze packed under the liver (16-33 s),
  needle holder and suture at the ligament / liver edge (38-60 s).
